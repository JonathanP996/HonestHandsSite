Your syllabus is the rulebook

It knows what your professor allows.

Add a class with its syllabus (PDF, Word, or pasted text). HonestHands pulls out the parts about AI and outside help, word for word, and shows them to you.

  • Catches pasted or reworded homework questions
  • Lets ordinary concept questions through
  • Reads screenshots of your homework, too
Private by design

The AI runs on your computer.

A small built-in AI does the checking, right on your computer. What you type is never sent to a server or a model provider.

  • One-time download, then it works offline
  • Answers in well under a second
  • No account needed to use it
Hard to get around

One browser for AI. Every other one is blocked.

Pick Chrome, Edge, Firefox or (on a Mac) Safari. Safari’s extension is built into the app, so you only switch it on. During a study session AI websites only work in that browser, and a tiny extension covers sites that hide their send button. The Claude and ChatGPT desktop apps are covered too.

Better together

Let a friend hold you to it.

Pair up with a friend, parent or mentor. Lock in for 30 minutes, an hour, or two, and the only ways out early are your accountability PIN or a friend releasing you.

  • They see: prompts you sent despite a warning, session times, daily counts
  • They never see: your syllabi, assignments, or clean and flagged messages
The Overridden Feed

Honest, not harsh.

When someone you watch sends a prompt despite a warning, it lands in your Overridden Feed. Give it a thumbs up if it looks fine, a thumbs down if it doesn’t, and tap Let’s talk about this to message them with the prompt already quoted.

And if the AI simply got it wrong, they can leave a note explaining what happened, so you see their side before you decide.

Drag it inOpen the downloaded file and drag HonestHands to your Applications folder.

macOS won’t open it?

Open Terminal, paste this, press Return, then open the app again:

Questions

Does it read everything I type?

Only what you’re about to send in an AI app or AI website, and only while a study session is on. It’s checked on your computer and never uploaded. If you create an account, only prompts you sent despite a warning, and counts, are shared with the friends you choose.

Can I still send a flagged message?

Yes. It’s a nudge, not a wall. Sending anyway is recorded as “overridden”, and that’s what a friend would see if you’ve paired up.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

It shouldn’t: the app is signed and notarized by Apple. If macOS still blocks it, use System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. Nothing is hidden: it’s a small program you can look through.

Does it have a dark mode?

Yes. HonestHands matches your system by default, and Settings lets you pick Light or Dark.

Does it work on Windows or phones?

It works on Mac and Windows computers. Phones aren’t covered yet.

Why does Windows warn me when I run the installer?

Windows shows a “Windows protected your PC” screen for any program that isn’t signed with a paid Windows certificate, and ours isn’t yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. We plan to sign it.

Can someone just quit it?

Outside a timed lock-in, yes: it’s honor system plus accountability. In a timed lock-in the app relaunches itself if it’s force-quit, and the only ways out early are your PIN or a friend releasing you. A determined person with Terminal can still get around it, and we’d rather say that plainly.
